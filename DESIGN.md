# DESIGN SYSTEM: Spike Worth (Radiant Tactical HUD)

## 1. Visual Theme: "Radiant Tactical HUD"
- **Aesthetic Direction**: High-octane tactical military & radyanite energy interface inspired by Valorant game UI and futuristic esports cockpits.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere**:
  - Background: Deep military obsidian `#080c14` with ambient crimson & cyan radial flares.
  - Scanlines & Bevels: Angular cut-corners (`clip-tactical`), subtle scanlines, and glowing perimeter accents.

---

## 2. Color Tokens
- **Valorant Crimson**: `#ff4655` - Primary CTAs, hot badges, accent borders.
- **Radiant Cyan**: `#00f5d4` - Secondary stats, live VP indicators, high MMR accents.
- **Ultra Gold**: `#fbbf24` - Champions, Arcane, and Exclusive limited items.
- **Tactical Dark**: `#0f1923` / `#161e2e` - Container panels with glassmorphism blur.

---

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Badges**: Inter / Sans Display in uppercase, tracked, bold weights (800-900).
- **Numbers & Currencies**: JetBrains Mono for jitter-free live currency conversions and countdowns.
